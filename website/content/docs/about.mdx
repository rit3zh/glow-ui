Reacticx is an open-source library of animated components for React Native. It
started in January 2025, out of the same frustration most component libraries
start from: more time was going into UI polish than into features.

The tools were never the problem. Reanimated, Gesture Handler and Skia can do
all of it. Assembling them into something that feels considered — the right
spring, the right delay, the press state that reads as physical — is the part
that took the afternoon, every time, in every project.

---

## What it is

A registry of components you **copy into your project**. `reacticx add` writes
the source into your repo, rewrites its imports to match your paths, and stops
there. No runtime package, no wrapper, no version to keep in step with.

That choice has one consequence worth being honest about: you own what you
copied. There is no upgrade that arrives for free. In exchange, nothing is
hidden behind an API surface someone else controls, and no styling override
turns into a fight — `reacticx diff` shows how far your copy has moved, and you
decide what to do about it.

---

## What it optimises for

- **Motion that survives contact with a real app.** Components are built on
  Reanimated and Skia and are tuned on device, not in a storybook.
- **Readable source.** The code is meant to be opened, understood and edited —
  it is documentation as much as it is a dependency.
- **Gestures first.** Drag, swipe, press and their interruptions are the design
  target, not an afterthought bolted onto a web component.
- **Staying out of the way.** No theme provider to adopt, no context to wire,
  no configuration ceremony before the first component renders.
